Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often about working with Scriptix. Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch.

What services does Scriptix offer? expand_more

We offer custom software development, AI automation, cybersecurity consulting, cloud strategy, UX/UI design, mobile app development, and data engineering. Most of our projects combine two or more of these — for example, a custom platform with AI-driven automation baked in, deployed on cloud infrastructure we help architect.

What industries do you work with? expand_more

We work across industries, with particular depth in fintech, healthcare, industrial, and retail. What matters more to us than the industry label is the shape of the problem — legacy systems that need modernizing, workflows that need automating, or products that need to be built from scratch.

What does your project process look like? expand_more

We follow a structured four-stage approach: Discovery, where we understand your goals and technical requirements in depth; Design, where we craft interfaces and system architecture; Development, using agile engineering with continuous integration and testing; and Deployment, with a seamless launch followed by ongoing support.

How long does a typical project take? expand_more

It depends entirely on scope — a focused automation workflow might take a few weeks, while a full custom platform build can run several months. During Discovery, we'll give you a concrete timeline estimate based on your specific requirements before any work begins.

How is pricing structured? expand_more

Pricing depends on project scope, complexity, and timeline, and is typically structured as either a fixed project fee or a time-and-materials engagement. We'll walk through the options that make sense for your project during our initial conversation, with full transparency before you commit to anything.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch? expand_more

Yes. Deployment isn't the end of our involvement — we offer ongoing support and optimization after launch, including monitoring, maintenance, and iterative improvements as your needs evolve.

Can you work with our existing team and systems? expand_more

Absolutely. Many of our engagements involve integrating with a client's existing engineering team, tools, and infrastructure rather than replacing them outright. We adapt to how you already work wherever it makes sense to.

How do you handle confidentiality and data security? expand_more

We treat client information and project details as confidential, and are happy to sign an NDA before detailed discussions begin. Security practices for any engagement are agreed as part of the project scope, tailored to what your data and systems require.

How do we get started? expand_more

Reach out through our contact page with a bit about your project. We'll schedule a conversation to understand your goals, and follow up with next steps and a proposed approach.

Still have questions?

We're happy to talk through your specific project and answer anything not covered here.
