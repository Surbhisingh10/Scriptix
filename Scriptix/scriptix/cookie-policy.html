Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are, and exactly what scriptix.in (the "Site") uses today. We've kept this page accurate to the Site's current, actual behaviour rather than generic boilerplate. If that changes, we'll update this page first.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They're commonly used to remember preferences, keep you signed in, or track behaviour for analytics and advertising.

2. What This Site Currently Uses

Scriptix.in does not currently set any first-party tracking or analytics cookies, and we do not currently use any advertising or marketing cookies. We also do not currently use browser local storage to track or store information about you.

3. Third-Party Fonts

The Site loads typefaces from Google Fonts so that text renders consistently across browsers and devices. Loading a font involves your browser making a request to Google's servers, which is subject to Google's own privacy practices, not ours. This integration does not set advertising or tracking cookies on our behalf.

4. If This Changes

If we introduce analytics, marketing, or other tracking technologies in the future, we will update this policy to describe them accurately, and where required by applicable law, provide a way for you to consent to or manage their use.

5. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Even though this Site doesn't currently set cookies, you can control cookies in general through your browser settings (including blocking or deleting them entirely). Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions specific to it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at business@scriptix.in.
