Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scriptix ("Scriptix," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit scriptix.in (the "Site") or otherwise interact with us. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and any message content when you submit our contact form or otherwise reach out. We may also collect standard technical information when you visit the Site, such as browser type, device type, and pages viewed, typically through server logs or similar mechanisms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, provide and improve our services, communicate with you about your projects or requests, and maintain the security and functionality of the Site. We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to these without your consent.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site uses limited technologies described in detail in our Cookie Policy. We encourage you to review that page to understand exactly what is and isn't used today.

4. Third-Party Services

The Site loads web fonts from Google Fonts to render text consistently. Loading these fonts involves a request to Google's servers, which is subject to Google's own privacy practices. We do not otherwise share your information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the Site or deliver our services, or when required to do so by law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at business@scriptix.in.

This page provides a general overview of our privacy practices and is not a substitute for legal advice. If you need this policy reviewed or adapted to specific regulatory requirements, please consult qualified legal counsel.
