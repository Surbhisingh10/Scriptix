Common Questions

Do I need to already be a Scriptix client to use this? expand_more

No, it works on its own. If we also built your site, fixes tend to move faster since we already know the codebase.

How long does SEO take to show results? expand_more

Usually a few months before you see real movement, longer in competitive niches. Anyone promising rankings in two weeks is either exaggerating or cutting corners that Google will eventually penalize.

Can you fix technical SEO issues directly in our code, not just suggest them? expand_more

Yes, as long as we have access to the codebase. That's the main reason we offer this alongside development instead of as a standalone service.

Do you offer local SEO services? expand_more

Yes: Google Business Profile optimization, location-specific pages, and the review and citation work that actually moves local search results.

Do you also manage paid ads and social media? expand_more

Yes. Paid search and social campaigns run alongside SEO, tied into the same analytics and automation, so everything's measured in one place instead of three tools that don't talk to each other.
